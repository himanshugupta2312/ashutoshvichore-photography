About

Tigers first. Then everything that flies, and every ridge worth climbing.

I'm Ashutosh. My lens is happiest in tiger country — long safari mornings, alarm calls in the undergrowth, and the rare, electric moment a tiger steps into the light. Tiger photography is the heart of what I do.

Between sightings I turn to birds — kingfishers, woodpeckers, meadowlarks — and to hiking, carrying a camera up mountain trails at dawn and dusk. I'm also a lawyer and a conservationist, and I use my pictures to help people care about the wild places that keep these animals alive.
